## Uses requiring written permission

Without separate written permission from Spark, you may not deploy, host, operate, or arrange for someone else to operate Rubotty:

- for a commercial purpose, including use intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation;
- for enterprise use, meaning use for or on behalf of a business or other commercial enterprise, including internal communications, business operations, customer service and managed services; or
- where you charge your users for access to or use of the instance, or for access to a community, product or service that the instance serves. This includes subscriptions, membership fees, admission fees, service charges and other required payments, whether or not Rubotty is separately priced.

These restrictions apply even if Rubotty is only one part of the offering, fees only recover costs, the operator makes no profit, or the operator is a non-commercial organization. For deployment, operation and access to Rubotty features, this addendum takes precedence over all permitted-purpose grants in the base licence, including Noncommercial Purposes, Personal Uses and Noncommercial Organizations. Calling an offering non-commercial does not authorize a use described above.

## No paid community access to Rubotty features

You must not deploy or use Rubotty in a community in which users are charged money for access to or use of Rubotty features. This prohibition applies whether the charge is imposed by you, a community owner or administrator, or another person acting for the community. You must not operate an instance that supplies Rubotty features to such a community.

A charge includes a direct feature fee, or a payment required to obtain, retain or increase access to Rubotty features through a subscription, membership, paid role, admission or bundle. It also includes charging to unlock additional use, higher limits or enhanced access to those features. Calling the payment a donation, cost contribution or fee for another part of the bundle does not avoid this prohibition when that payment is required for access to or use of Rubotty features. Charges solely for unrelated goods or services do not trigger this section where Rubotty feature access, capabilities and limits do not depend on payment; the separate commercial, enterprise and paid-community permission requirements above still apply.

This prohibition applies even if some users receive the features free, the operator or community makes no profit, or the operator has general permission for commercial or enterprise operation. Such general permission does not waive this separate prohibition. An exception requires a separate written agreement from Spark that expressly permits the otherwise prohibited paid community access to Rubotty features.

## No charges to communities for instance use

An instance operator must not charge a community, or its owner, administrator or representative, for access to or use of the operator's Rubotty instance. An instance operator is a person or entity responsible for deploying, administering or providing that instance, directly or through someone acting on its behalf.

This prohibition includes setup, connection, subscription, usage and service fees, and required payments described as hosting, administration, support, donations or cost reimbursement, when the payment is a condition of obtaining, retaining or increasing the community's access to or use of the instance. Bundling that access into another paid service does not avoid the prohibition. It applies even when the community does not charge its own users and the operator only recovers costs or makes no profit.

General permission for commercial or enterprise operation does not waive this separate prohibition. An exception requires a separate written agreement from Spark that expressly permits charging communities for access to or use of the instance. Paying an independent general-purpose infrastructure provider for compute, storage or networking does not by itself make that provider a Rubotty instance operator or constitute charging a community for Rubotty access.
