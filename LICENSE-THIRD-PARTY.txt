## Runtime component receipt

The image build writes `/usr/local/share/rubotty/runtime-components.json` after installing the interpreters and Lua rocks. It records the probed Ruby, Lua, LuaRocks and available Fennel/Teal versions, the installed rocks and the exact source commit used for Teal. Its companion `teal-source.json` is captured before the temporary checkout is removed. These records travel with `/usr/local` into the runtime image.

When preparing a release SBOM, extract this receipt from the same validated image and run `ruby scripts/runtime-components.rb enrich scanner.cdx.json runtime-components.json release.cdx.json`. Retain the scanner output and receipt alongside the enriched output; the enrichment preserves existing components, adds missed runtimes and rocks, and records the receipt digest. This does not certify a different rebuild or the source tree alone.
